## Born in 1996. Raised in a not so small town (aka New Delhi) in India. Working hard since 2013.

### What I am doing currently
Hi, my name is Aayush Malik. Currently I am working as a **Product Manager** at Planet Labs, the world's largest satellite imaging company that images earth daily to help save life on Earth. I design here the High-Resolution Satellite Tasking Platform so that our customers can get access to the data when they need it to make the decisions impacting our world.

### What have I done previously
Previously I have worked with **International Initiative for Impact Evaluation, Harvard School of Public Health, Tata Centre for Development at UChicago, and American India Foundation**. I am interested in the applications of **Evidence-based Decision Making**, **Geospatial Analytics**, and **Causal Inference** for the wider benefit of business community that want to work using more data, and less gut enzymes.

### Know more about me
I call myself a **highly motivated self-starter** because I have been able to learn many of the things I use at my work on my own. This includes learning Python, German, and Geospatial Analytics. I am a Product Manager who got into the field before being a researcher, data analyst, programmer, and designer.

My hobbies include learning languages, taking adventure trips, and exploring the culture of old cities across the world. I am a firm believer of functional minimalism and functional design.
